Description:
  A companion volume to Bram Stoker's Dracula, built chapter by chapter across
  a first reading of the novel. It opens with "Of the Papers" — a sumptuous
  catalogue of the novel's in-world source documents (Harker's travel notebook,
  Mina's journal, Seward's phonograph cylinders, Lucy's letters, the Demeter's
  log, the newspaper cuttings, the telegrams, Van Helsing's casebook), each
  with a mock artifact photograph — followed by a large numbered map of the
  whole journey with map notes keyed to the chapters.

  For each of the novel's 28 chapters: a packet of documents in the style of
  1893 print — hotel notices, guidebook extracts, pamphlets, broadsides,
  diligence timetables, transcribed letters, itemized bills, newspaper
  cuttings, telegrams — plus oil-painting-style plates and antique-style maps,
  all set in a red-cloth, crimson-and-gold design.

  Stoker's novel (1897) is in the public domain; every document and illustration
  in this companion is newly made for it. Read it alongside the novel, chapter
  by chapter — or browse it as a cabinet of Victorian curiosities.

  415 pages. A5 format.
